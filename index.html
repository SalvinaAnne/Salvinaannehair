Personalised Consultation

During your consultation, we take the time to understand your hair goals, lifestyle and desired look to ensure your extensions are perfectly tailored to you. We’ll assess your natural hair type, colour, density and scalp health while discussing the best method — whether Weft or Tape — to achieve comfortable, long-lasting results.

You’ll receive professional guidance on the most suitable extension method, length and volume recommendations, custom colour matching, pricing, installation and maintenance schedules, plus aftercare advice to keep your extensions looking beautiful.

Meet Me

Meet
Sammy

Hi, I’m Samantha, the founder and educator behind Salvina Anne Hair Extensions — a name that represents strength, growth, and everything I’ve poured my heart into.

My passion for hair began when I first got extensions at sixteen. I instantly fell in love with how they made me feel — confident, feminine, and complete. But I also realised something important: most clients weren’t being fully educated on the different methods, maintenance, or proper aftercare. That discovery sparked my mission to do things differently.

Since I was seventeen, I’ve personally struggled with slow hair growth, so I know exactly how it feels to want long, healthy, beautiful hair that still feels like your own. That personal journey inspired me to create a space where women could feel educated, cared for, and genuinely understood.

At Salvina Anne Hair Extensions, I specialise in luxury Weft and Tape methods, offering a tailored experience that’s one-on-one, relaxed, and built on trust. Every detail matters — from the consultation to the final blend — because your confidence is at the heart of what I do.

Beyond my client work, I’m passionate about training students who are eager to master the art of hair extensions. Through my Signature Training Programs, I teach hands-on techniques, product knowledge, and the professional standards that define the Salvina Anne approach — helping the next generation of stylists build confidence and skill.

Salvina Anne Hair Extensions was born from passion, experience, and the belief that beauty is deeply personal. Here, it’s not just about changing how you look — it’s about how you feel.

Please Read Before Your Appointment

Policies & Procedures

Please take a moment to review before your appointment.

Payments

  • All payments for Hair Extensions must be paid in full before extensions can be ordered.
  • Remaining service balance must be paid on the day of service.
  • All services are non-refundable.

Bookings & Cancellations

  • Cancellations or reschedules must be made with at least 48 hours' notice.
  • Late arrivals of more than 15 minutes may need to be rescheduled.

Hair Extensions

  • All Hair Extensions are non-refundable once installed or ordered.
  • Any issues must be addressed at the time of service.
  • Any slipping or significant dropping of extensions can be fixed within 1 week of installation or maintenance, free of charge.
  • Any concerns after this period will be reviewed at your next scheduled maintenance appointment.

Maintenance & Aftercare

  • Clients must follow the recommended aftercare to ensure the longevity of their extensions.
  • Incorrect maintenance or the use of unapproved products may void any complimentary fixes or warranties.
  • Regular maintenance appointments are essential to keep extensions secure, healthy, and looking their best.

General Etiquette

  • Please arrive with clean, dry hair free of product buildup.
  • Respectful communication and punctuality are appreciated to ensure a smooth experience for all clients.
